Mythos Mash

Another game I worked on is Mythos Mash a game about Mythology characters fighting mythological creatures and trying to fight Hades: God of the Underworld and his minions, after Hades has created a lifeform like no other to fight different gods. You start off with Zues and Anubis who team up to find other gods to beat Hades. Some charcters I've had ideas for is Sun Wukong, Amaterasu and I've even though about adding historical figures to fight alongside them, including Temujin (Ghengis Khan), Bruce Lee, and Blackbeard. As this was my first project it's not as good, but this is when I found out that I hate programming as I had to program an array for the first time, which was not fun. With this project I practiced game balancing with allowing certain heros to have abilities that others didn't. This made it easier for players to discern what character did what and what role they served in battle.

Plague and the 7 Syringes

Plague and the 7 Syringes is a 2D Platform, Run and Gun game based on Middle Age Plague Doctors. The game follows Plague, a doctor who died in his past life and now has to go through the 7 rings of Hell to gain different powers and gain another life. The Middle Ages is my favorites historical period next to the Prohibition Era and I thought it would be a cool idea to make a game about a Plague Doctor who has to fight for another life after dying to the black plague. I used construct 3 which made some things a little harder than usual. It's not the most polished thing, but for my favorite project, I think I did quite well. This passion project is something I would hope to continue later on when I'm a better game developer/designer. This was my first shot at 2D level design, it was my second project ever, so it obviously isn't the most polished thing ever, but for what it was I think I did really good.

Eldritch Incident:1948

This game named "Eldritch Incident: 1948" is about the aftermath of WWII and how the raid of Unit 731 went but when they got there they found bodies upon bodies. The main researcher on this project is named Nikolas Kazenkov and he did horrible research on people and space. He one day found a portal and a creature came out and killed everyone there and fled to Russia. Nikolas notifies other country leaders and they send a group of people to go kill the eldritch beings. I was the Lead Game Designer for this project initialy I wanted to do a rougelike with this game in mind but didn't know how it would fit into the overall theme of the game, so I decided to make a turn-based narrative game that had fun gameplay aspects, like, the ability to cross moves with one another to create a bigger move, and a synergy meter to determine how certain characters fight with one another.

For our senior year our instructor wanted everyone in our class to participate in the SkillsUSA Interactive Application and Game Development competition. So I decided I wanted to start ambiitious. I gathered a group of my friends and we started production on a game called Overwrought. Out of my entire class, we were the only ones to start a 3D game and for its quality I thought we did pretty well. This game follows Eric a guy in his 20s going back to his childhood house. Play as Eric as he goes into his memories to get a fresh set of eyes on his past, and relive past events. This game has a similar concept to The Backrooms where every level feels liminal but has their own unique mechanic to go along with the level.

As the end of my senior year approached we learned a new engine, Godot. So for our final project of our senior year we had to make a 3D game. This games is called The Backwoods, it's about a 14 year old kid named Sam who has lost his ball in the backwoods, your goal is to go find his ball, but something lurks in the woods, waiting for you. This game is supposed to represent how people don't know who lives around them and what their story is. The Patient is an idea that I've had for a while and this project made him shine. This game is a narrative horror game and the story quickly unfolds as you dive deeper to find your ball.
