Who am I?

I am Zachary Reed, and I am a senior in the Gaming & Multimedia class at Robert H. Gibson Technical School and a student at Rensselaer City School District. I am a cunning and active game designer, sound designer, and creative writer. My love for this field started when I was a child, building houses in Minecraft using diamonds, emeralds, and gold, to designing my own games, creating, mixing, and mastering sound effects for games/creative projects.
